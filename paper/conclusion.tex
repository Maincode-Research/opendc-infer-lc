\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{OpenDC-Infer-LC}, an open, hardware-neutral benchmark that
measures long-context and RAG inference serving as \emph{SLO-qualified goodput}
over the full serving system, rather than as peak tokens per second. Across two MoE
tiers, two vendors (AMD MI355X and NVIDIA B200), four backends, three precisions,
and up to 64 GPUs, one message holds: \emph{serving capacity is governed by where
the prefill and tail-latency bottlenecks fall, and by the maturity of the serving
stack---backend, quantization kernel, and routing policy---far more than by raw
accelerator FLOPS}. The same metric also exposes wins that raw throughput hides,
such as a small MoE that is fast but answers cached-RAG queries incorrectly. We
release the reference harness, the frozen hash-pinned prompt sets, and the exact
cluster run scripts so that every result is reproducible.
